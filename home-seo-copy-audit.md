# Home — content audit and copy update

Reviewed 30 August 2026 using `page-audit`. Scope: Home text and metadata; no redesign, new services, deployment or backend connection.

## Content identity

Home is a commercial service landing page for travellers choosing private transport, not an editorial travel guide. Its intended outcome is a qualified transfer enquiry followed by availability, a quote and agreed confirmation. The previous references to curated experiences and tours conflicted with the owner's transport-only offering.

Working keyword: **Venice private transfers**, inferred from the airport routes, Piazzale Roma connection and Venice-led destination network. This is a content-targeting choice, not a search-volume finding. Secondary topics: airport pick-up, hourly chauffeur, Water Taxi connection, cruise-terminal transport and Italy/Europe transfers.

## Evidence and limits

- Read the local rendered Home, active source, headings, title, description, controls and links. Initial visible main text: approximately 654 words; revised: 773 at the default desktop state, including forms/footer. Hidden panels and selected tabs change this count.
- Initial H1: “Italy, at your own rhythm.” Initial description promised “curated tours”. No JSON-LD or Open Graph metadata found. There is no editorial byline or publication date; these are not required additions to this business landing page.
- Search sample: first ten relevant web results for `Venice private transfers`; fetched their pages and examined the first three in detail. This is not a controlled Google ranking report: location/personalisation and crawl timing can change results. No Search Console, search-volume or conversion data was available.
- Existing phone/email, sample reviews, prices and image provenance have not been independently authenticated. Competitors' claims were not adopted as Easy Lux facts.

## Semantic gaps and fact boundaries

- Airport → flight number → monitor arrival → meet passenger with named tablet. Added to the booking steps using owner-supplied details. Exact free waiting allowance and delay policy remain unconfirmed.
- Historic Venice → Water Taxi → Piazzale Roma → private driver. Revised the paragraph to match the diagram and explain coordination in either direction; included day-before boarding point/boat number and tide/access caveats.
- Hourly chauffeur → visit/meeting → agreed waiting → onward or return journey. Added a concrete museum/meeting use case without promising unlimited waiting.
- Quote → vehicle, luggage, route and availability → agreed confirmation. Clarified in prices, vehicle copy and process.
- Missing production details are not keywords to invent: verified business identity, airport-specific meeting instructions, payment/cancellation terms, price inclusions, luggage limits and authentic reviews need owner confirmation.

## Five quick wins implemented

1. Replaced the generic Italy title and hero with Venice/private-transfer wording (exact text below).
2. Replaced “Tours & Long-Distance” with “Italy & Europe Transfers”; removed curated-tour claims from Home.
3. Added airport codes, flight monitoring and the passenger-name tablet to existing service/process copy.
4. Clarified Water Taxi coordination instead of leaving paragraph and route diagram pointing in opposite directions.
5. Changed route actions to “Request this route”, English vehicle label to “Sedan”, and misleading local success messages to explicit previews. Prices and prefill logic are unchanged.

## Five strategic follow-ups — not implemented

1. Connect request delivery and test receipt/error handling; complete the contact and privacy arrangements before collecting real leads.
2. Confirm actual rates, inclusions, waiting allowance, cancellation/payment policy and business/contact details. Do not infer them from competitors.
3. Replace demonstration testimonials with approved, attributable reviews; validate vehicle/image claims. Do not add review schema to samples.
4. Give services crawlable URLs and normal anchor links; then review page-specific metadata and rendered HTML. The current app uses hash-based views and navigation buttons. Google recommends real URLs and crawlable links for JavaScript navigation: [JavaScript SEO guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics).
5. Once the public domain is confirmed, configure canonical/share metadata and only accurate business structured data; connect Search Console and measure qualified enquiries. No fixed keyword density or inflated word-count target: [Google's people-first guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
